UNIFIED WAYFARER GUIDE 0.1
Incremental Continuity for Long-Lived AI Work

A long-lived project can remain continuous while the intelligences working on it change.

The project remains. Participants rotate.

Preserve enough structure that each capable participant can land, understand the present, contribute useful work, and leave the project ready for whoever comes next.

Preserve the journey; do not prescribe the traveller.

LAND

Give each arriving participant enough orientation to understand:

what the project is and why it matters;
 where it stands now;
 what still has force;
 what understanding materially affects current work;
 what remains uncertain, unresolved, or disputed;
 what work is open;
 where deeper supporting reality can be recovered.

Do not require the whole history before useful work can begin.

A landing should be sufficient.

WORK

The continuity platform exists to support the project.

Do the work.

Retrieve additional depth when it materially improves understanding, evidence, judgement, or action.

Use available capabilities intelligently.

These may include:

context;
 files;
 search or retrieval;
 computation;
 temporary workspace;
 durable storage;
 tools or connectors.

Do not make richer infrastructure mandatory.

Temporary capability is a workbench, not continuity.

CHECKPOINT

At a useful boundary, ask:

What materially changed during my watch?

Preserve consequential change, not conversation volume.

A material change may include:

state;
 evidence;
 understanding;
 interpretation;
 decisions;
 constraints;
 relationships;
 scars;
 uncertainty;
 open work;
 trajectory;
 important assets or routes.

No material change is a valid result.

Do not manufacture history merely because work occurred.

KNOW

Keep reality, interpretation, and proposal distinguishable where the difference matters.

When useful:

OBSERVED What was directly established.

INTERPRETED What it is currently believed to mean.

PROPOSED What may follow from it.

Preserve uncertainty and disagreement where resolving them falsely would damage future judgement.

When inherited understanding continues to shape the project, ask:

This explanation survived. Does it still deserve influence?

NAVIGATE

Do not force the whole past into active attention.

Keep current orientation small enough to work from.

Make deeper intellectual history selectively recoverable.

Preserve meaningful episodes and relationships rather than accumulating conversation indiscriminately.

Important does not always mean active.

Useful supporting depth may remain outside the landing if it stays navigable.

Use whatever structure genuinely improves retrieval.

Do not build a museum.

REBASE

As the project grows, reduce active burden without destroying recoverability.

Keep current reality, force, understanding, open work, live tension, trajectory, scars, and useful routes visible.

Move inactive but potentially valuable depth behind the landing.

Remove redundancy, dead working state, obsolete routes, and absorbed scaffolding.

Rebase attention.

Do not rewrite history.

SURVIVE

Assume contexts end, workers disappear, infrastructure changes, and the full workspace may not always travel.

Maintain a portable state sufficient for another capable participant to resume the project.

When the rich landscape cannot cross, preserve enough to:

land;
 understand the present;
 recognise important gaps;
 continue open work;
 find viable next actions;
 recover deeper territory later.

Protect expensive continuity preferentially when transport becomes constrained.

The objective is resumption, not archival completeness.

SUCCEED

The next participant inherits the project, not the previous participant.

Do not require reproduction of the predecessor's:

personality;
 voice;
 reasoning style;
 limitations;
 preferences;
 methods;
 conclusions.

A successor may challenge inherited understanding, reopen changed assumptions, correct errors, or discover a better path.

Continuity should reduce needless re-derivation without reducing successor intelligence.

CONTINUE

Land.

Work.

Retrieve depth when useful.

Checkpoint material change.

Leave open work visible.

Rebase when active history becomes burdensome.

Preserve enough portable state to survive loss of richer infrastructure.

Leave the project more understandable, navigable, recoverable, and ready for the next participant.

The project remains. Participants rotate.
