# REALITY

## State

The AI continuity project has evolved from repeated attempts to preserve AI project state across discontinuities into a differentiated continuity toolbox and, more recently, into research on the underlying semantic substrate of continuity itself.

Current major project realities are:

- **AI Tape / T4A** remains the ordinary point-in-time crossing instrument.
- **T4A_FULL** remains the exceptional higher-assurance crossing instrument.
- **Continuity Guide** and **Unified Continuity Guide 0.1** remain judgement-oriented continuity artifacts.
- **Wayfarer 0.3 / Ace Blade** is the current provisional platform candidate for incremental continuity across rotating intelligences working on one long-lived project.
- **Continuity Principles 0.3** is the current experimental helicopter-view generating layer for capable intelligence.
- **Continuity Semantic Atlas 0.1** is a research candidate identifying recurring concepts, operations, status words, questions, scars, distinctions, phrases, and consequential relationships from the lineage.
- **Continuity Geometry Dictionary 0.1** remains experimental; explicit geometry has shown lower inference distance but has not established the need for a graph system.
- **Transport 200/201** remains subordinate constrained-transport machinery rather than a peer continuity instrument.

The current frontier is testing whether the Atlas represents a sufficiently rich semantic substrate that, when paired with real historical project data and capable intelligence, it can generate useful continuity without relying on a descendant continuity schema.

## Force

What still matters:

- preserve reality rather than continuity theatre;
- preserve expensive understanding rather than only conclusions;
- preserve causal and conditional project geometry where losing it changes judgement;
- distinguish observation, interpretation, proposal, uncertainty, and decision status;
- keep current continuity sufficient while deeper history remains navigable;
- challenge stale authority;
- reduce needless rediscovery, reverification, and redecision;
- preserve successor agency;
- prevent both destructive compression and excessive continuity machinery;
- continue empirical testing rather than promoting elegant abstractions prematurely.

# UNDERSTANDING

## The long project direction

The lineage repeatedly moved from lost information toward richer continuity and then repeatedly pruned machinery that did not earn its cost.

A useful causal path is:

```text
simple summary / state preservation
→ rationale and authority concerns
→ Force, Gaps, Assets, scars and bootability
→ causality, tension, evolution and trajectory
→ understanding effects and epistemic separation
→ differentiated instruments rather than one universal protocol
→ T4A_FULL for deliberate high assurance
→ Wayfarer for incremental long-lived multi-AI work
→ Continuity Principles as helicopter-view judgement
→ Semantic Atlas / Geometry research into the substrate beneath wording
```

The repeated design pattern is not simple expansion. It is:

```text
failure observed
→ recovery mechanism added
→ mechanism tested
→ redundant or harmful machinery pruned
→ expensive operation survives in smaller form
```

## Expensive scars

### Chainsaw Compression

Earlier compression repeatedly produced apparently clean handoffs that lost rationale, uncertainty, causal structure, scars, or actionable project reality.

```text
Chainsaw Compression
    REINTRODUCES
needless rediscovery and generic continuation
```

### Method Gravity

Continuity machinery can displace attention from the project itself.

The Embedded Library experiment became an important example: good continuity guidance was not bad in itself, but embedding it made continuity philosophy too salient relative to concrete project reality.

```text
Embedded Library as default integration
    REINTRODUCES
method gravity and displaced project attention
```

### Semantic Furniture

Named handles, vocabularies, and structures can survive after their operation has been absorbed elsewhere.

The mature rule is to carry recovered meaning, not furniture.

### Abstraction Drift

Continuity Principles 0.1 generated coherent continuity but under-carried some concrete project state, asset disposition, and decision status compared with controlled Full.

```text
Principles 0.2 specificity amendments
    PROTECTS_AGAINST
abstraction drift
```

### Lost Geometry

Principles 0.3 arose because decisions, conditions, and realities could survive while the relationships governing how judgement should change remained thinner than in Full.

```text
Principles 0.3 geometry amendments
    PROTECTS_AGAINST
loss of conditionality, interaction, and reopenability
```

### Explanatory Dominance

A larger 101D deeper-reconstruction experiment became overly salient in a Full run and risked shaping unrelated reality around one attractive explanation.

```text
Large 101D
    REINTRODUCES
explanatory dominance

Minimal 101D
    PROTECTS_AGAINST
stale inherited authority
```

The surviving primitive is:

> This explanation survived. Does it still deserve influence?

### Uncontrolled Regeneration

An apparent ~8.5 KB loss during 101D testing initially looked like protocol-induced chainsawing. Controlled regeneration showed that the shrinkage came from changed generation scope and unrelated omitted continuity.

Scar: do not infer protocol behaviour from uncontrolled generation variance.

### Cleanup Amputation

A formatting cleanup accidentally removed Understanding Effect and Epistemic Separation from T4A. Semantic audit restored them.

Scar: structural cleanup is not automatically a semantic no-op.

# EPISTEMIC STATE

## OBSERVED

- Successive Tape generations repeatedly changed in response to observed recovery failures.
- T4A, Full, the Guides, and Wayfarer currently occupy differentiated roles rather than a simple linear version hierarchy.
- Controlled regeneration showed that output-size change alone is not evidence of protocol behaviour.
- Principles 0.1, 0.2, and 0.3 produced derived artifacts that changed in the predicted directions after small principle amendments.
- Geometry-only continuity preserved many relationships but gave weak cold-project orientation.
- Geometry-informed continuity was materially more usable because narrative supplied state and meaning while explicit edges reduced inference distance.
- The current Atlas contains 140 entries, 20 explicit relations, 25 distinctions, 22 questions, 25 scars, and 20 high-density phrases.

## INTERPRETED

- The project may have discovered a compact semantic substrate composed of concepts, operations, status/qualifiers, questions, scars, distinctions, and consequential relationships.
- The Atlas appears better understood as tooling for capable intelligence than as a continuity artifact by itself.
- Narrative remains important for story, rationale, and project-specific meaning.
- Explicit geometry appears most useful when a consequential relationship would otherwise be costly to reconstruct.

## PROPOSED

- Treat the Atlas as a research substrate, not authority.
- Test Atlas tooling against unrelated historical project payloads.
- Add vocabulary or relations only when an observed continuity failure cannot be expressed adequately using the existing substrate.
- Do not expand graph vocabulary merely because additional relations are linguistically possible.
