The project has matured from building continuity instruments bottom-up to testing whether the design intelligence behind those instruments can itself be preserved as a generating layer for capable AI.

- **Wayfarer 0.3 / Ace Blade candidate:** bootable incremental project continuity for long-lived work across rotating intelligences.
- **Transport 200/201:** subordinate constrained-transport capability used inside relevant instruments rather than a separate peer instrument.

The current frontier is **Continuity Principles 0.3**, a helicopter-view attempt to capture the common continuity intelligence from which a strong AI can derive an appropriate artifact, environment, assurance method, or incremental continuity behaviour without being instructed to imitate an existing tool.

0.3 adds project geometry to 0.2. It asks continuity to preserve not only concrete reality and decision status, but also the conditions that keep decisions sensible, the interactions between surviving realities, and the circumstances that should reopen judgement.

# WHAT HAS FORCE NOW

1. **Reality outranks elegance.** A coherent abstraction is not sufficient if the successor must rediscover the concrete project underneath it.
2. **Strong AI should derive structure from the situation.** The Principles carry judgement, not descendant anatomy.
3. **The existing toolbox remains active.** The Principles are not yet a replacement for T4A, Full, Wayfarer, or the Guides.
4. **Continuity quality is measured by avoided needless re-derivation without loss of reality.**
5. **Decision status matters.** A successor should know whether an idea is adopted, provisional, rejected, superseded, or open.
6. **Decision conditionality matters.** A successor should know what conditions keep a material judgement sensible and what changed conditions ought to reopen it.
7. **Interactions matter.** Preserving A and B is insufficient when the consequential relationship A→B is what makes them actionable.
8. **Successor freedom remains fundamental.** Past conclusions are evidence rather than inherited authority.
9. **Supporting depth may remain outside active attention.** Current continuity should be sufficient while deeper reality remains navigable.
10. **Higher consequence may warrant higher assurance.** Multiple views or verification are justified by risk, not ritual.
11. **Portable continuity must survive loss of richer capability.**
12. **New structure must earn its cognitive cost.** The project has scars from both destructive compression and excessive machinery.
13. **Stability is valid.** A mature system need not change merely because another attractive idea exists.

# EXPENSIVE UNDERSTANDING

## Three distinct continuity failure surfaces have emerged

### Information / chainsaw loss

Rich project state can be compressed into fluent but generic continuity that drops rationale, uncertainty, causal structure, scars, relationships, or viable action.

### Machinery gravity

The reaction to information loss can itself become harmful. Schema, reports, maps, semantic furniture, auditors, and procedures can consume attention that should remain on the project.

### Abstraction drift

The first Principles-generated artifact exposed a subtler failure. Design understanding survived strongly while concrete project specificity became thinner.

The project learned:

> **Conceptual understanding is not sufficient continuity if important project reality must still be rediscovered.**

The practical test became:

> What would the successor otherwise have to **rediscover, reverify, or decide again**?

Principles 0.2 responded by preserving decision disposition, concrete active assets, causal history, and an operational completion check.

## The remaining geometry problem

Comparison of the 0.2 artifact with controlled Full suggested that most major content classes now survived. What remained thinner was the geometry between them:

```text
Why is this currently true?
What condition keeps it true?
What should cause reconsideration?
What else depends on it?
What breaks or changes when it changes?
```

Principles 0.3 therefore adds two rules:

> **Where material decisions depend on conditions, preserve the dependency well enough for changed conditions to reopen judgement.**

> **Preserve interactions whose loss would materially change how surviving realities should be understood, judged, or acted upon.**

This is intended to preserve deformation rules of the project, not merely a snapshot of its pieces.

# DECISION LANDSCAPE

## T4A remains the ordinary crossing instrument

**Status:** Adopted / current.

**Reason:** It provides a strong bootable point-in-time crossing with optional deeper recovery and low enough machinery cost for routine handover.

**Depends on:** T4A continuing to provide adequate routine crossings without recurrent material continuity loss.

**Reopen judgement if:** ordinary T4A crossings repeatedly require higher-assurance reconstruction, lose important project geometry, or become less efficient than a different derived continuity form.

**Interaction:** T4A's leanness is protected partly by leaving long-lived incremental project continuity to Wayfarer and higher-assurance triangulation to Full. If those boundaries collapse, T4A's role should be reconsidered.

## T4A_FULL remains exceptional

**Status:** Adopted / provisional in use, not default.

**Reason:** Consequential crossings can require deliberately independent views to reduce omission, misunderstanding, and successor-misapplication risk.

**Depends on:** ordinary T4A remaining sufficient for routine crossings and independent passes continuing to add material assurance rather than paraphrase.

**Reopen judgement if:** routine crossings begin showing recurrent losses that Full reliably prevents, or if Full's independent passes cease adding distinct value.

**Interaction:** Full's value is assurance, not volume. Turning Full into the normal path would increase cognitive cost and could recreate method gravity.

## Wayfarer owns incremental multi-AI project continuity

**Status:** Wayfarer 0.3 / Ace Blade is provisional and under trial; its distinct job is accepted conceptually, its current implementation is not yet ratified.

**Reason:** Repeated point-in-time reconstruction is not always the best model for one long-lived project worked on by rotating intelligences.

**Core time model:**

```text
The project remains.
Participants rotate.
```

**Core question:**

> **What materially changed during my watch?**

**Depends on:** incremental maintenance of an inherited project landscape being materially better for long-running work than fully reconstructing project continuity at every transition.

**Reopen judgement if:** repeated watches accumulate sediment, participants rewrite full state anyway, the platform becomes maintenance-heavy, or repeated T4A crossings provide equivalent continuity with lower operational burden.

**Interaction:** Wayfarer must remain bootable and portable enough not to become dependent on rich workspace infrastructure. Its Portable Checkpoint exists because capability-rich continuity without graceful degradation would be fragile.

## Continuity Principles remain experimental

**Status:** 0.3 is the current experimental generating-layer candidate.

**Reason:** 0.1 demonstrated schema-independent regeneration of major continuity capabilities; 0.2 materially improved project specificity after abstraction drift was identified; 0.3 now targets conditionality, interactions, and reopenability.

**Depends on:** strong AI being able to convert the principles into continuity appropriate to the actual problem without unacceptable loss relative to specialised tools.

**Reopen or reject if:** foreign-project tests generate abstract but operationally weak artifacts; material project geometry still disappears; constrained environments fail to remain bootable; or specialised instruments consistently outperform the Principles by enough to justify their additional structure.

**Interaction:** The Principles must not become protected by their own elegance. Their applicability rule applies to themselves.

# CONSEQUENTIAL INTERACTIONS

These relationships materially affect how the project should be judged.

## T4A ↔ Full

T4A stays lean partly because Full exists for crossings where independent assurance is worth its cost.

If T4A absorbed Full's assurance machinery, routine crossing cost would rise.

If Full lost independent challenge and became merely longer T4A, its reason to exist would weaken.

## T4A ↔ Wayfarer

T4A handles point-in-time crossing.

Wayfarer handles persistent project continuity across rotating participants.

If Wayfarer rewrites full useful state every watch, it loses its incremental advantage and converges toward repeated T4A generation.

If T4A tries to maintain a persistent project landscape, it begins absorbing Wayfarer's job and risks bloat.

## Wayfarer ↔ Portable Checkpoint

Wayfarer may exploit richer project environments.

Portable Checkpoint prevents that richness from becoming fragility.

If checkpoint scope expands toward the entire landscape, it becomes a mini-Wayfarer and recreates the transport problem.

If checkpoint scope becomes too thin, the next participant cannot resume the project independently.

## Principles ↔ specialised tools

The Principles attempt to preserve design intelligence above the toolbox.

Specialised tools remain engineered implementations with known strengths.

If Principles-derived continuity approaches specialised recovery quality with lower structure, that supports the generating-layer hypothesis.

If important reality repeatedly disappears, descendant structure contains irreducible operational value and should remain preferred for that situation.

The Principles therefore supplement rather than automatically supersede the tools.

## Rebase ↔ recoverability

Reducing active burden is useful only if demoted material remains recoverable when it could matter again.

If rebase removes unique rationale, evidence, scars, or conditions, it becomes chainsaw compression.

If nothing is ever demoted, continuity accumulates until attention itself becomes the bottleneck.
