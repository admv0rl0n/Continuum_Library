# CURRENT ORIENTATION

This project explores how useful intellectual work can survive discontinuities between AI systems, sessions, contexts, tools, and infrastructure without pretending that subjective identity persists.

The work has matured from a search for a single continuity protocol into a small family of specialised instruments plus a new top-down attempt to identify the principles from which appropriate continuity behaviour can be derived.

Current instrument roles are approximately:

- Continuity Guide: richer continuity judgement and design intelligence.
- Unified Continuity Guide: compact crossing-oriented principles for capable intelligence.
- T4A: ordinary point-in-time crossing.
- T4A_FULL: higher-assurance point-in-time crossing using independent perspectives.
- Wayfarer 0.3 / Ace Blade candidate: incremental continuity for a long-lived project worked on by rotating intelligences.
- Continuity Principles 0.1: candidate common generating layer above the specialised instruments.

The immediate frontier is no longer primarily another Tape or Wayfarer revision. It is testing whether Continuity Principles 0.1 contains enough design intelligence for a capable AI to derive an appropriate continuity solution without being told which existing instrument to imitate.

# WHAT STILL HAS FORCE

Several project commitments remain strongly active:

1. **Trust intelligence.** Strong AI should be given truthful reality, expensive understanding, epistemic boundaries, and enough freedom to derive appropriate structure.
2. **Useful continuation beats formal compliance.** A structurally perfect artifact that loses live reality has failed.
3. **Continuity is not identity.** The successor inherits work, not subjective selfhood.
4. **Smallness is not automatically success.** Compression can destroy rationale, causal structure, scars, uncertainty, and actionability.
5. **More machinery is not automatically success.** Protocol, maps, categories, reports, and tooling can consume the attention they were intended to preserve.
6. **Successor freedom matters.** Past conclusions are evidence, not authority.
7. **Important does not always mean active.** Deeper source reality can remain recoverable without occupying current attention.
8. **Portable continuity must survive loss of richer infrastructure.** Temporary compute and rich workspaces are useful but cannot be hidden requirements.
9. **Changes must earn their keep.** New structure should address a real continuity failure rather than aesthetic completeness.
10. **Stability is a valid outcome.** Nothing requires another branch or version if the current tools already cover the failure surface.

# EXPENSIVE UNDERSTANDING

## Two opposite continuity failures

The project repeatedly encountered paired dangers.

One side loses continuity through excessive reduction:

- chainsaw compression;
- missing rationale;
- flattened uncertainty;
- lost relationships;
- generic handoffs;
- false completeness.

The other side loses continuity through excessive machinery:

- procedural scaffolding;
- semantic furniture;
- archive gravity;
- map tourism;
- compliance theatre;
- methods becoming more salient than project reality.

The correct response is not a fixed midpoint. It is contextual judgement about what reality and recoverability the actual situation requires.

## Scars are valuable, but not permanent law

Rejected paths and expensive lessons should survive when their loss would cause needless re-derivation.

However, preservation can create its own failure if a historically valid explanation continues to govern after its conditions change.

The surviving primitive is:

> **This explanation survived. Does it still deserve influence?**

This arose through Research 3 and was eventually squeezed from a much larger deeper-reconstruction proposal into a minimal applicability check.

## Repeated full reconstruction is not always the right time model

T4A naturally treats continuity as a crossing: recover what must survive from one state/context to another.

Wayfarer clarified a different geometry for long-running work:

> **The project remains. Participants rotate.**

Its governing question became:

> **What materially changed during my watch?**

This supports incremental project continuity rather than repeatedly reconstructing the entire useful project state at every AI transition.

## Continuity can be active without containing the entire territory

A recent Wayfarer-derived addition to T4A captured a useful general boundary:

> Keep the primary continuity state sufficient.
> Make useful supporting depth navigable from it.

The important distinction is between a sufficient landing and deeper recoverable territory. This reduces pressure either to cram everything into the handoff or to orphan source material outside it.

## Higher stakes justify higher assurance, not automatic complexity

T4A_FULL demonstrated that consequential crossings can warrant independent perspectives because omission, misunderstanding, and successor misapplication are different failure modes.

The top-down principle is broader than Full itself:

> When consequence or uncertainty makes one reconstruction insufficient, increase assurance through independent recovery, alternative interpretations, source verification, or additional perspectives.

Do not multiply views merely to manufacture confidence.
