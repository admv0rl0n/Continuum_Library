# EXPENSIVE UNDERSTANDING

## The project learned two opposite failure surfaces

Early continuity work repeatedly discovered that aggressive summarisation could produce fluent, generic artifacts that lost rationale, scars, uncertainty, causality, relationships, or viable continuation. This became the **chainsaw compression** scar.

The response could fail in the opposite direction. Additional schema, auditors, semantic furniture, maps, passes, and continuity machinery could consume the attention they were intended to protect. This became the **machinery / method-gravity** scar.

The project therefore does not seek a fixed midpoint between “small” and “large.” It seeks the amount and shape of continuity required by the actual problem.

## A third failure has now appeared: abstraction drift

The first artifact generated from Continuity Principles 0.1 was coherent, bootable, epistemically careful, and rich in design understanding. It did not look like classic chainsaw compression.

However, comparison with the controlled T4A_FULL handoff found that it knew the project’s *why* better than some of its *exactly what, which, where, and current status*.

This exposed a different failure geometry:

```text
Project reality
↓
Design understanding
↓
Coherent abstraction
↓
Concrete working specificity begins to fade
```

The new scar is:

> **Conceptual understanding is not sufficient continuity if important project reality must still be rediscovered.**

Continuity should therefore be tested by asking what the successor would otherwise need to **rediscover, reverify, or decide again**.

## Decision disposition is continuity

It is often insufficient to remember that an idea existed. The successor may need to know whether it is:

- adopted;
- provisional;
- rejected;
- superseded;
- still open.

Losing that status can force unnecessary re-evaluation even when the underlying concept survived.

## Historical explanation is not permanent authority

Research 3 exposed that a preserved explanation can remain historically valid after its original conditions changed.

The surviving primitive is:

> **This explanation survived. Does it still deserve influence?**

This was backported into ordinary T4A only after a larger deeper-reconstruction proposal was narrowed through redundancy review, an interpretive-dominance warning, and controlled regeneration.

## Crossing and persistence are different time models

T4A naturally asks:

> What must survive this crossing?

Wayfarer found a distinct ownership question for a long-lived project:

> **What materially changed during my watch?**

Wayfarer’s time model is:

```text
The project remains.
Participants rotate.
```

Its purpose is incremental multi-AI project continuity rather than repeated full reconstruction at every transition.

## A sufficient landing and deeper territory can coexist

Wayfarer’s navigation work yielded a small lesson useful enough to backport into T4A:

> Keep the primary tape sufficient.
> Make useful supporting depth navigable from the tape.

This resolves a false choice between cramming everything into the active handoff and losing discoverability of valuable source depth.

## Higher assurance is contextual

T4A_FULL demonstrated that omission, loss of meaning, and successor misapplication are different risks. For consequential crossings, one view may be insufficient.

At helicopter altitude, the lesson is not “always make three reports.” It is:

> Increase assurance when the cost of omission, misunderstanding, or misapplication warrants it, using independent recovery, alternative interpretations, source verification, or additional perspectives when those materially improve confidence.

- The controlled T4A_FULL continuity artifact was approximately 35.7 KB.

## INTERPRETED

- Principles 0.1 successfully carried much of the stack’s **continuity capability**, but suffered some **abstraction drift** in project-specific detail.
- The 15 KB result therefore should not be treated as equivalent to Full merely because it remained coherent and bootable.
- The missing material appears more like 3–6 KB of high-value specificity plus Full’s intentionally independent assurance views than 20 KB of wholly essential lost reality.
- Principles 0.2 is a targeted response: preserve decision disposition, explicitly reject abstraction as sufficient continuity, and require an operational completion check.

## PROPOSED

- Judge improvement by reduced rediscovery/reverification/redecision cost, not by byte count.
