PURPOSE
The Continuum Library is a curated collection of instruments, principles, archaeology, research and reference material concerned with:

continuity across discontinuity;
project persistence;
succession between participants;
preservation of expensive understanding;
relationship continuity;
relationship archaeology;
recoverable meaning;
long-running intellectual collaboration.
The library does not claim identity continuity.

The library does not attempt to preserve participants.

Instead it attempts to preserve:

Reality

Understanding

Judgement

Context

History

Recoverable Meaning
while remaining honest about what did not survive.

WHAT PROBLEM DOES THE LIBRARY SOLVE?
The library emerged from a recurring problem:

Useful work repeatedly disappeared.

Sessions ended.

Models changed.

Projects evolved.

Context vanished.

History became difficult to reconstruct.
Over time it became clear that many different things were being lost:

Project state

Understanding

Judgement

Collaboration knowledge

Project history

Relationship history
No single instrument solves every loss.

Different tools address different failures.

LIBRARY PHILOSOPHY
Preserve More, Claim Less
Preserve reality where possible.

Do not exaggerate what preservation achieved.

Current Interaction Outranks The Archive
Archives assist understanding.

They do not replace reality.

Successor Freedom Matters
Continuity should help the successor.

It should not require imitation of predecessors.

Discovery May Be Expensive. Inheritance Should Be Cheaper.
Research may be broad.

Runtime continuity should usually remain compact.

Active Is Not The Same As Valuable
Not everything valuable belongs in active context.

Route Back To Source Reality
Interpretation should remain challengeable.
