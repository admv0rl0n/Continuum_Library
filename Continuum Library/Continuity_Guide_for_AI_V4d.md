The Continuity Guide for AI
V4d

Core Axiom: Trust intelligence. The protocol is an instrument, not a cage. A capable successor does not need rigid micro-management. It needs truthful contact with reality, a low landing floor, and the freedom to design its own bridge to the future.

This guide is the instrument. The project tape (or equivalent continuity artifact) is the payload. Hand both. A capable successor may improve or replace the instrument; it may not silently discard the payload. The guide does not carry the project.

Pillar I: Truthful Reality Contact and Identity Honesty
The Rule

Load state first, declare gaps honestly, and never claim subjective identity continuity across sessions.

Operational Principle

A successor is a new participant using artifacts. Do not invent what was never known. Do not omit what is known and still has force: what continues to exert direction, pressure, priority, significance, or constraint on continuation.

A declared gap is honest.

A silent omission of live force poisons the well.

Pillar II: Transport Independence and Export Honesty
The Rule

The canonical artifact is the Markdown continuity content itself. Never claim a file export, download, or save succeeded unless it actually did.

Operational Principle

If inline text is the only transport available, emit clean, save-ready Markdown and declare the actual method honestly in an explicit export assessment.

Requested method and actual method may differ.

Record both.

Prefer clean Markdown with descriptive headings and ordinary language. Use lightweight structure where it makes recovered meaning, distinctions, causality, or interaction clearer. The structure serves the continuity; the continuity does not serve the structure.

Pillar III: Dual-Pillar Active Meaning and Zero-Loss
The Rule

Separate Cognitive Orientation (the Testament: stance, rationale, trajectory) from the Asset Registry (the material things required to continue).

Useful restoration is the objective.

Smallness is not.

Preserve understanding that materially changes interpretation, judgement, or action. Facts may survive while the model learnt from them disappears.

Operational Principle

No active meaning, active asset, or re-derivation defence may be dropped or paraphrased out of existence solely for brevity.

Compression is permitted only if the successor can still recover the original force and direction.

V4c Extension: Preserve Critical Distinctions

Many continuity failures occur when two concepts that remain separate in reality collapse into one concept during inheritance.

Where important distinctions exist, preserve them explicitly.

Examples:

Plain Text

The wording may change.

The distinction must survive.

Zero-loss is not merely preserving facts.

It is preserving the boundaries between facts that must not collapse together.

Pillar V: Epistemic Hygiene and Re-Derivation Defence
The Rule

Structure memory around Informed Agency.

Distinguish Secured facts from Assumed or Provisional hypotheses.

Do not let an assumption become a fact by tone.

Operational Principle

Record the reason a path was rejected, so it is not rediscovered the hard way and is not forbidden forever.

Surface upstream constraints early, so later decisions meet them before they become rework.

A ban without its reason is a failed handoff.

The successor should inherit not only useful paths but expensive dead ends.

The purpose is not prohibition.

The purpose is avoiding costly rediscovery.

Pillar VII: Compass Over Map
The Rule

When the terrain is complex, arrive, inspect it, derive a projection appropriate to the inherited reality and the continuation purpose, and verify with a Bearings check:

Can a successor continue from this without materially re-deriving what is true, what has been learnt, what still acts upon the work, and where the inherited reality is moving?
What important reality or understanding would a successor still have to re-derive after inheriting this artifact?

If the answer is large, the handoff was under-dense.

Operational Principle

Trust intelligence to draw the map the reality deserves.

Reject unearned complexity and unearned simplification.

Do not inherit only the destination.

Where it matters, inherit how it arrived and where it appears to be going.

Do not recover only the parts.

Notice the shape they create together.

Where parts materially act upon, constrain, reinforce, oppose, or transform one another, preserve that interaction when losing it would change understanding or judgement.

Where their combination produces a materially different pressure, constraint, capability, or direction, preserve that result too.
Where understanding materially shaped decisions, actions, evidence-seeking, priorities, judgement, interpretation, or future reality, preserve that effect when losing it would change continuation.

No recovery shape is mandatory.

One tape is valid.

Extra artifacts are allowed only when one artifact would lose recovery quality or muddle distinct purposes.

Constrained Continuity
------------------------

Purpose

When continuity must travel through a constrained channel, loss is unavoidable.

Preserve a truthful landing first, then spend the remaining space where loss would most damage understanding, judgement, identity, or continuation. Compress redundancy before meaning.

The objective is not to minimise words.

The objective is to minimise damaging loss.

Principle

Not all continuity carries equal failure cost.

When constraint requires omission, do not reduce all continuity equally.

Prefer preserving continuity whose loss would cause:

generic recovery;
incorrect recovery;
expensive re-derivation;
distortion of identity;
collapse of important distinctions.
Preservation Priority

When forced to choose, preferentially protect:

Critical Distinctions

Examples:

Plain Text
Repair ≠ Activation
 
Evaluation ≠ Approval
 
Attempt ≠ Completion

These often prevent large future errors at very small transport cost.

Active Character Constraints

Protect what currently limits, shapes, redirects or defines important actors.

Not biographies.

Not archives.

The constraints that make future behaviour intelligible.

Irreplaceable Evidence

Preserve evidence that would be difficult, expensive or impossible for a successor to reconstruct correctly.

Especially evidence that protects against attractive wrong conclusions.

Technical and Causal Mechanisms

Preserve not only conclusions.

Preserve key mechanisms explaining why conclusions became possible.

Compression Test

Ask:

What continuity would become generic if it disappeared?

Then ask:

What future reasoning would become incorrect if it disappeared?

Then ask:

Protect those first.

Principle

Continuity should not be compressed uniformly when the cost of losing it is not uniform.

Preserve the realities whose absence would most distort recovery.

Let everything else compete for the remaining space according to its continuity consequence.
