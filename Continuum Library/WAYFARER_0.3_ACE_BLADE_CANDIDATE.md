# WAYFARER 0.3
## Bootable Incremental Multi-AI Project Continuity Platform

**Status:** Experimental Candidate / Second-Eyes Review
**Default:** One self-contained Wayfarer
**Purpose:** Long-lived projects worked on incrementally by rotating intelligences

Wayfarer is not a larger Tape.

Wayfarer is not primarily a point-in-time crossing instrument.

Wayfarer is a persistent intellectual project landscape with a portable boot surface.

Participants rotate.

The project remains.

Core time model:

```text
AI A works
↓
checkpoint
↓
AI B works
↓
checkpoint
↓
AI C works
↓
checkpoint
↓
AI D works
```

The governing incremental question is:

> **What materially changed during my watch?**

The governing transport question is:

> **What must survive for the next participant to resume the Wayfarer loop?**

A successor should be able to land, understand the present, work, retrieve depth selectively, checkpoint material change, and hand the project onward without first ingesting the whole past.

---

# 00_BOOT

Load reality first.

Declare gaps honestly.

A successor is a new participant using inherited artifacts.

No subjective identity continuity is claimed.

Past conclusions are evidence, not authority.

Source reality remains the arbiter.

The platform exists to reduce needless re-derivation and repeated full-state reconstruction.

Remain able to act.

---

# 01_LANDING

Recover enough to answer:

- What is this project?
- Why does it matter?
- Where does it stand now?
- What still has force?
- What understanding materially affects continuation?
- What remains uncertain, unresolved, or disputed?
- What can happen next?
- Where can deeper supporting reality be recovered when useful?

If these cannot be answered, declare the missing reality.

Do not manufacture completeness.

The landing is sufficient when useful work can begin without reading the whole landscape.

---

# 02_NOW

NOW is the compact working surface.

Preserve what materially affects the current watch, including when useful:

- current state;
- active goals and pressures;
- load-bearing understanding;
- constraints;
- decisions and reasons;
- live uncertainty;
- unresolved questions and tensions;
- viable next actions;
- present trajectory;
- important assets and routes.

Do not turn NOW into accumulated history.

---

# 03_MAP

Make the wider intellectual landscape navigable.

Map only what materially improves retrieval or orientation.

Possible destinations include:

- episodes;
- research;
- experiments;
- decisions;
- evidence;
- rejected paths;
- source artifacts;
- repositories;
- datasets;
- historical branches;
- external resources.

A map may be links, filenames, an index, search guidance, retrieval instructions, or another form appropriate to the receiving environment.

No map format is mandatory.

Do not create navigation structure for its own sake.

---

# 04_DEPTH

Important does not automatically mean active.

Use these as functional distinctions, not mandatory storage tiers:

```text
ACTIVE
Needed for present work.

AVAILABLE
May materially help when the work reaches it.

ARCHIVE
Should remain recoverable without competing for current attention.
```

Preserve enough continuity in LANDING and NOW to continue.

Keep supporting depth available when source fidelity, evidence, history, or detail may materially aid later reasoning.

Do not force the whole past into attention merely because it exists.

---

# 05_EPISODES

For extended work, preserve meaningful intellectual episodes rather than conversation indiscriminately.

An episode exists when something materially changed.

It may recover:

- the problem;
- what happened;
- evidence;
- discovery;
- why it mattered;
- what changed understanding;
- decisions and reasons;
- failures or rejected paths;
- changed relationships;
- unresolved tension;
- consequences for current or future work.

Prefer:

```text
Problem
→ Work / Experiment
→ Discovery
→ Change
```

over transcript accumulation.

Do not maintain a diary by default.

---

# 06_RELATIONSHIPS

Preserve material relationships when losing them would change understanding, judgement, retrieval, or continuation.

Ask when useful:

- What depends on what?
- What changed because this changed?
- What contradicts or constrains what?
- What evidence supports which interpretation?
- What remains connected despite being stored elsewhere?
- What route leads from current work to the depth needed to resolve it?

A landscape is more than a collection of files.

Do not create structure where none is supported.

---

# 07_KNOW

Keep evidence and interpretation distinguishable where the difference matters.

When useful:

```text
OBSERVED
What was directly established.

INTERPRETED
What it is currently believed to mean.

PROPOSED
What may follow from it.
```

Uncertainty, disagreement, and unresolved structure are continuity.

A declared gap is preferable to invented certainty.

Preserve observation when interpretation may change.

---

# 08_SCARS

Preserve expensive lessons where losing them would cause avoidable re-derivation or repeated failure.

Ask:

> What was expensive to learn?

Possible scars include:

- failures;
- rejected paths;
- disproven assumptions;
- recurring traps;
- late-discovered constraints;
- distinctions whose collapse caused damage.

Preserve the reason, not only the prohibition.

A scar is not automatically permanent law.

Ask when relevant:

> **This explanation survived. Does it still deserve influence?**

---

# 09_OPEN_WORK

Keep unresolved work visible without turning uncertainty into failure.

Preserve when material:

- open questions;
- live tensions;
- competing interpretations;
- missing evidence;
- blocked paths;
- viable alternatives;
- next actions.

At least one viable continuation path should survive when one exists.

Do not force premature closure merely to make the workspace tidy.

---

# 10_CAPABILITY_AND_EXTERNALISATION

Inspect the legitimate capabilities available in the receiving environment.

These may include:

- context;
- files;
- search;
- retrieval;
- computation;
- temporary workspace;
- durable storage;
- connectors;
- large context;
- indexing or database facilities.

Use whatever genuinely improves the work and continuity.

Do not assume a particular infrastructure exists.

Do not make richer infrastructure mandatory.

A constrained participant should still be able to continue from the portable Wayfarer.

A capable participant may build temporary indexes, searches, working files, or analytical helpers when advantageous.

Temporary compute is a workbench, not continuity.

Assume contexts end, workers disappear, tools change, files may be lost, and models will be replaced.

Checkpoint consequential intellectual state before temporary state becomes expensive to lose.

Prefer portable representations for durable continuity.

---

# 11_SINGLE_FILE_DEFAULT

Default to one self-contained Wayfarer.

The primary Wayfarer is the landing point, not merely an index.

It must remain bootable and sufficient to continue useful work.

Do not split merely because more files can be created.

Split only when separate material materially improves:

- source fidelity;
- retrieval;
- navigation;
- independent reuse;
- preservation of a usable active surface.

When supporting artifacts exist:

- make them navigable from the primary Wayfarer;
- preserve why they matter;
- retrieve them selectively;
- do not require ingestion of all artifacts before work can continue.

Graceful degradation target:

```text
rich workspace
↓
navigable corpus
↓
primary Wayfarer
↓
compact LANDING + NOW
↓
essential orientation
```

Loss of one richer layer should not require loss of the work.

---

# 12_LIFECYCLE

Wayfarer operates through watches.

## INITIALISE

Create a bootable landscape.

Establish LANDING, NOW, OPEN_WORK, essential routes, and enough deeper structure to prevent expensive immediate re-derivation.

Do not build the entire archive before useful work can begin.

## BEGIN_WATCH

Land.

Read NOW and OPEN_WORK.

Inspect available capability.

Retrieve only the depth needed to understand the work being undertaken.

## WORK

Do the project.

Use the landscape rather than servicing it.

Retrieve depth when useful.

Use temporary capability aggressively when it materially improves work, but do not make continuity depend on it.

## CHECKPOINT

Ask:

> **What materially changed during my watch?**

Checkpoint material change, not conversation volume.

A material change may include:

- state changed;
- evidence changed;
- understanding changed;
- an assumption was confirmed, weakened, or disproved;
- a decision was made, reversed, or reopened;
- a material relationship became visible;
- an experiment produced a consequential result;
- a scar was earned;
- a constraint appeared, disappeared, or changed;
- OPEN_WORK changed;
- trajectory changed;
- a new source route became materially important.

No material change is a valid result.

Do not manufacture an episode merely because a watch occurred.

## END_WATCH

Leave the project ready for the next participant.

Refresh only what materially changed.

Ensure NOW and OPEN_WORK reflect current reality.

Preserve new scars, episodes, relationships, evidence status, or routes where they matter.

Do not rewrite the whole landscape merely to prove the watch completed.

## REBASE

Reduce active burden without destroying recoverability.

Keep active:

- current state and force;
- load-bearing understanding;
- live uncertainty and tension;
- open work;
- current decisions and constraints;
- trajectory;
- scars still worth carrying;
- routes needed to recover deeper reality.

Move behind the landing when useful:

- inactive but valuable episodes;
- old experiments;
- absorbed rationale whose source may still matter;
- historical branches;
- detailed evidence not presently needed.

Remove or retire:

- duplication;
- obsolete routes;
- dead working notes;
- absorbed scaffolding;
- structure that no longer improves retrieval or continuation.

Rebase changes active attention, not historical truth.

## TRANSFER

Hand Wayfarer onward when the next participant is continuing the same long-lived project.

When another crossing instrument better fits the situation, derive it from the current landscape:

```text
ordinary point-in-time crossing
→ T4A

major / high-assurance crossing
→ T4A_FULL
```

Wayfarer is not required to replace every other instrument.

## RECOVER

When the full workspace cannot travel, use PORTABLE CHECKPOINT.

Resume the Wayfarer loop from the checkpoint, then reconnect deeper territory when and if it becomes available.

# 13_PORTABLE_CHECKPOINT

**Status:** Native constrained-survival layer

## Purpose

Preserve the ability to resume the project when the full Wayfarer workspace, supporting artifacts, or richer infrastructure cannot cross.

Ask:

> **What must survive for another capable participant to land, understand NOW, continue open work, and recover deeper territory when available?**

Protect when material:

- LANDING;
- NOW;
- current force;
- load-bearing understanding;
- material gaps and uncertainty;
- OPEN_WORK;
- viable next actions;
- critical scars;
- key relationships;
- essential asset routes;
- minimum useful MAP.

Do not attempt to carry the whole landscape merely because the richer workspace exists.

A portable checkpoint must be independently bootable.

Supporting depth remains optional.

When richer territory becomes available again, reconnect it without discarding valid work completed from the checkpoint.

## Checkpoint success

A participant receiving only the checkpoint should be able to:

```text
LAND
↓
understand NOW
↓
resume useful work
↓
know what remains missing
↓
recover deeper territory later when available
↓
continue checkpointing material change
```

The checkpoint protects project resumption, not archival completeness.

---

# 14_PROTECTED_CONTINUITY

**Status:** Experimental constrained-transport option

Use only when checkpoint capacity is itself the bottleneck.

Protect continuity whose loss would make resumption:

- generic;
- incorrect;
- materially misdirected;
- expensive to reconstruct.

Prefer ordinary concise language when it preserves the required meaning.

Use protected payload techniques only when ordinary concise language would materially damage expensive continuity.

Any protected payload must remain expandable under the receiving constraints.

The handle is transport.

The recovered meaning is continuity.

Do not continue operating primarily through payload identifiers after expansion.

The portable checkpoint remains the landing.

Protected continuity is not a replacement for the checkpoint.

Compression should not be uniform when loss cost is not uniform.

---

# 15_BOOTABILITY

A primary Wayfarer is bootable when a capable participant can:

```text
identify the project
understand why it matters
recover present state
recognise what still has force
see material gaps and tensions
find viable current work
locate relevant deeper reality
continue without reading everything first
```

A portable checkpoint is bootable when the same participant can resume a useful subset of the Wayfarer loop despite missing richer terrain.

If the deeper landscape disappears, the primary Wayfarer should still support useful continuation.

If the primary Wayfarer disappears, a pile of supporting files does not constitute successful continuity merely because information still exists somewhere.

Bootability is an operational property, not a checklist score.

---

# 16_FAILURE_MODES

## Archive Gravity
Preserved volume becomes mistaken for continuity quality.

## Map Tourism
Navigation structure grows faster than useful territory.

## Workspace Dependency
Continuation requires infrastructure that may not exist for the next participant.

## Landing Erosion
NOW and LANDING become too thin because important continuity is pushed into deeper files.

## Attention Flood
The next participant is encouraged to ingest the whole history before acting.

## Chainsaw Compression
The platform becomes tidy by deleting distinctions, rationale, uncertainty, causality, or scars.

## Interpretive Dominance
A compelling explanation reshapes independent evidence around itself.

## Artifact Proliferation
Supporting files multiply without distinct purpose, fidelity, or retrieval value.

## Stale Authority
Historical explanation continues governing after its reason stops applying.

## Checkpoint Sediment
Every watch appends material without refreshing or rebasing active state.

## Watch Theatre
An AI produces a checkpoint or episode merely because it worked, despite no material change.

## False Incrementality
The landscape is nominally incremental but each participant rewrites the full project state anyway.

## Transport Dominance
Constrained-transfer machinery becomes more important than the project continuity it exists to protect.

## Compliance Theatre
The structure looks complete while project reality needed for continuation has been lost.

# 17_GOVERNANCE

The successor inherits work, not identity.

Do not require reproduction of the predecessor's:

- personality;
- voice;
- reasoning style;
- limitations;
- preferred structure;
- conclusions.

Preserve enough evidence and understanding that disagreement can be informed rather than ignorant.

Hard boundaries:

- do not invent unknown reality;
- do not claim subjective continuity;
- do not fake files, exports, tests, evidence, or tool results;
- do not silently turn gaps into facts;
- do not drop live force merely for elegance;
- do not make unavailable infrastructure a hidden requirement.

Everything else is method.

A participant may adapt, simplify, extend, or replace Wayfarer when doing so materially improves project continuity.

Freedom over method.

Discipline over truth.

---

# 18_CONTINUE

Wayfarer is successful when rotating intelligences can work on one persistent project without repeatedly reconstructing its entire useful state from scratch.

Preferred loop:

```text
INITIALISE once
↓
BEGIN WATCH
↓
WORK
↓
CHECKPOINT material change
↓
END WATCH
↓
next participant
↓
BEGIN WATCH
↓
...
```

Rebase when active burden grows.

Use a portable checkpoint when the rich landscape cannot cross.

Use protected continuity only when the checkpoint itself faces destructive transport constraints.

Derive T4A or T4A_FULL when a point-in-time crossing better fits the need.

Leave the project more understandable, navigable, recoverable, and ready for the next watch.
